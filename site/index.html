Features

Everything a tidy desktop needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

Tessera is a small native program. It draws your icons in groups, steps aside when you work, and puts everything back exactly as it was when you quit.

Groups that sort themselves

On first run your icons are gathered by kind: apps, folders, documents and system items. New files land in the right group automatically. Move, resize, roll up and rename groups however you like.

Tabs

Put Work, Play and Study in one group and switch with a click. Drop an icon on a tab to file it there.

Real drag and drop

Drag between groups, out to Explorer, or in from anywhere. Drop links from your browser and onto folders or the Recycle Bin.

Folder portals

Show any folder live on your desktop, like Downloads or a project, and browse into its subfolders in place.

Profiles

Keep complete desktop arrangements for Work and Gaming, and switch with a hotkey.

Colours from your wallpaper

Tessera finds your wallpaper's main colours. Give every group one of them, or switch on Follow my wallpaper and they recolour themselves whenever you change it.

Keyboard first

Arrows, typing to find an icon, F2, Del, Ctrl+C/V and Ctrl+Shift+N work just like on the normal desktop.

Multi-select

Ctrl+click, Shift+click and Ctrl+A. Then drag, copy or delete them all at once.

Themes

Nine built-in looks, plus your own. Save, share and import them as files.

Projects

Make it yours

Glass, grain and gradients.
Your call.

Every group can have its own material, border, shadow, font, title style, icon effects and hover motion. You see each change live, on the desktop, as you make it.

Acrylic: your wallpaper, softly blurred under a tint, with a touch of grain.

Light as air

Designed to cost you nothing.

Tessera only draws when something changes. When your desktop is still, it does nothing at all.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

Download

Click Download for Windows to get tessera-setup.exe, the latest release from GitHub.

Install

Run it. It installs for your account only, with no administrator prompt. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed yet: choose More info → Run anyway.

Enjoy

Your icons are grouped by kind straight away. Right-click a group, or double-click the tray icon, to make it yours.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Tessera is free and open source under the MIT License. There are no ads, no trials and no paid tier.

Does it change or move my files?

No. Arranging icons in groups never touches your files. Only actions you ask for, such as deleting, renaming or dropping files in, do what they would do on the normal desktop, through Windows' own file operations.

What if I uninstall it?

Uninstall from Settings → Apps. Your normal desktop icons come back as they were. You choose whether to keep or delete your saved groups.

Which Windows versions?

Windows 10 and 11. The settings window uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 11 already includes.

Will it slow my PC down?

No. Tessera uses no CPU while your desktop is still and about 20 MB of memory. Effects such as blur and shadows are drawn once, not every frame, and there's a low-effects mode for older PCs.
