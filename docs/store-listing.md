## Description (≤ 10,000)

```
A tidy desktop, without giving up your desktop.

Tessera gathers the icons on your Windows desktop into calm, translucent groups that sit right on your wallpaper. Apps in one place, folders in another, documents where you can find them. It sorts them for you the first time, then files new things into the right group automatically.

Your files stay exactly where they are. Tessera only changes how your icons are shown, never where they live. Quit Tessera and your normal desktop comes straight back.

ORGANISED YOUR WAY
• Groups you can move, resize, roll up, rename and sort.
• Tabs: put Work, Play and Study in one group and switch with a click.
• Folder portals: show any folder, like Downloads or a project, live on your desktop and browse into it.
• Profiles: keep complete arrangements for work and for gaming, and switch with a hotkey.

WORKS LIKE THE DESKTOP YOU KNOW
• Double-click to open, right-click for the usual Windows menu.
• Drag icons between groups, out to File Explorer, or in from anywhere. Drop links from your browser to make shortcuts.
• Keyboard shortcuts: arrows, typing to jump to an icon, F2 to rename, Delete, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V and Ctrl+Shift+N.
• Select several icons with Ctrl+click, Shift+click or Ctrl+A.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Materials: Acrylic, Frosted glass, Mica, Gradient, Solid or simple tinted.
• Borders, soft shadows, corner radius, title styles (plain, pill or underline), fonts, label styles and icon effects.
• Smooth hover and selection motion, or none at all.
• Colours from your wallpaper: Tessera finds your wallpaper's main colours. Use them for every group, or turn on Follow my wallpaper and your groups recolour themselves whenever you change it.
• Nine built-in themes, plus your own. Save, share and import them.

LIGHT AS AIR
Tessera is a small native program. It uses no CPU while your desktop is still, about 20 MB of memory, and has a low-effects mode for older PCs. Your groups stay visible when you press Win+D, and step aside when you work.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no telemetry and no network access. Your layout is saved only on your PC.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Tessera is free and open source under the MIT License, made by Hashan Kannangara. The code is on GitHub for anyone to read, improve and share.
```
